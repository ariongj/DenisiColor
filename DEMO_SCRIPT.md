# Meeting walkthrough — Denisi Color demo (≈ 15–20 minutes)

**Links:** storefront https://ariongj.github.io/DenisiColor/ · CMS https://ariongj.github.io/DenisiColor/admin (login
pre-filled, click "Hyr"). Locally: `npm run dev` → http://localhost:5173 and /admin.

**Setup:** two windows side by side, the storefront on the left and the CMS on the right. Beforehand run
**Konfigurimet → Të dhënat demo → Rivendos** so the demo data is dated today.

## 1. Their shop, online (4 min)
- Their logo and navy brand, Albanian by default (switch to English in the top bar).
- Hero: "Vegla, ndriçim dhe veshje pune": delivery across Kosovo, order by message, Bosch / Kapriol / PARKSIDE /
  Novaled, open Mon–Sat 07–19.
- Seven categories built from their own Facebook posts, plus the **Ngjyra & fasada** tile and page (enquiry form).
- "Porosia në katër hapa" explains ordering, then the blog ("Këshilla") with articles about LED colour, IP ratings
  and LED profiles.
- "Na shkruani" opens WhatsApp with their number. The footer shows the real address, phone, e-mail and hours.

## 2. Shopping like a customer (4 min)
- **Veshje pune → Jakne Softshell 26 €**: pick a colour, add to cart. Add 3 workwear pieces and the automatic −10 %
  appears. Try the code `MIRESEVINI10`.
- **Ndriçim → Ndriçues tavani 36W**: no price yet, so it shows **"Kërko çmimin"**. Send the enquiry form, or tap
  WhatsApp (the message is pre-filled with the product name).
- Checkout: Kosovo cities and delivery zones, cash on delivery / bank transfer / card (simulated) → confirmation `DC-…`.

## 3. The "wow" moment (1 min)
- The order appears **instantly** in the CMS (bell, toast, overview numbers).
- The price enquiry lands in **Kontaktet**, ready to turn into a **B2B quote** (Oferta) with prices.

## 4. The CMS (7 min)
- **Përmbledhje**: sales, new orders, low stock, new contacts and "Kërkojnë vëmendje".
- **Porositë**: order detail, prepare shipment, partial refund, print invoice; **Draftet** for phone/WhatsApp orders.
- **Produktet**: add their real prices, sizes and photos; a product can be a normal item or "Kërko çmimin".
  **Koleksionet** (Bosch, Për kantier, Sezoni i dimrit), **Inventari**, **Furnizimet**.
- **Zbritjet**: −10 % from 3 workwear pieces, free delivery over 50 €, the `KANTIER` code for contractors, and the
  winter offer scheduled ahead.
- **Kontaktet → Ofertat**: B2B quotes, e.g. 40 LED ceiling lights for a new building in Ferizaj, or extinguishers for
  an office in Prishtina.
- **Online Store**: theme, slideshow and banners, homepage editor with draft → preview → publish.
- **Konfigurimet**: contact data, delivery zones and fees, payments, languages, staff & roles.

## 5. What we need from Denisi Color to go live
- The full product list: prices (with or without VAT), sizes and colours, stock.
- Clean product photos and permission to use them, plus the original logo file.
- Delivery fees and times, return/warranty terms, business number (NUI/TVSH), bank account.
- Which paint/facade products to sell online.
- Card payment (which bank), courier, fiscalisation (ATK), and a domain.

Tip: if anything gets messy during the demo, **Konfigurimet → Të dhënat demo → Rivendos** restores everything.
